Our Story

We're not an agency.
We're a shop family.

My father Oscar has been turning wrenches his whole life. Baez Auto Repair in Elgin, IL is how our family paid the mortgage, took trips, bought cars, and built something real from nothing.

I watched him lose leads because there was no system. Calls that didn't convert. Estimates that went cold. Customers who never came back. So I built the tools to fix it — a dashboard, an analytics system, ad tracking tied to real repair orders.

Now we help other independent shops do the same. We know what an RO is. We know how parts ordering works. We know what actually matters at the end of the week.
