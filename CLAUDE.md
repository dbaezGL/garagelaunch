## Outreach Strategy

- **Primary close method:** In-person — Danny drives to shops
- **Sequence:** Call first to confirm owner is there and find slowest time → show up with printed one-pager → text as follow-up → Facebook DM as another channel
- **Geography:** Start local (Elgin, DeKalb, Aurora, suburbs). Remote delivery is possible — can scale anywhere.
- **Tracker:** `docs/outreach-tracker.csv`

### Cold Call Script

> "Hey, is this the owner? ... Hey, my name's Danny, I handle marketing for Baez Auto Repair in Elgin — it's my family's shop. I built our whole online presence from the inside and now I help other independent shops do the same. Do you have a couple minutes?"

**If yes:** "Do you guys have a website right now?"
- No website: "That's why I'm calling — most customers search on their phone before they ever call. If I get you 5 extra customers a month, at an average repair of $400 that's $2,000 back in your pocket. And if it doesn't work in 60 days, I'll refund you. No risk."
- Has website: "How's it working — are you actually getting calls from it?"

**To close:** "I'm not selling you anything right now — I just want to show you what I did for our shop. Can I stop by this week or would a call work better?"

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already have someone" | "Are you happy with what you're getting? I noticed a few things online that might be costing you calls." |
| "No budget" | "That's exactly who I built this for. And with the guarantee, worst case you get your money back. What's 5 extra customers a month worth to you?" |
| "Send me something" | "What's the best number to text you? I'll send it right now." (get cell, follow up same day) |
| "How do I know it works?" | "Baez Auto Repair is my family's shop in Elgin — I built our presence from scratch. Real shop, real numbers." |
| "Too busy" | "What's your slowest hour this week? I'll work around you." |
| Not interested | "No problem. Keep my number if anything changes." Click. |

## Key Principles

1. **Speak directly to the shop owner.** He's busy, skeptical, and has been burned by agencies before. Be direct, be real, no corporate language.
2. **The story is the pitch.** Baez Auto Repair is the case study. Real numbers, real shop, real results.
3. **Done beats perfect.** Ship fast, iterate based on real feedback.
4. **Don't oversell scope.** Only offer what can actually be delivered right now.
