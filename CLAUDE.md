## What Garage Launch Is

A services business targeting **single to 3-man independent auto repair shops** — shops that are starting out or need modernization. Most have no website, no Google presence, and are surviving on word of mouth alone.

**The angle:** Most marketing agencies have never touched a wrench. Danny grew up in the shop, runs one, and built real tools for it. That's the differentiator.

**The story:** Baez Auto Repair is the proof of concept — real shop, real data, real results.

## Key Principles

1. **Speak directly to the shop owner.** He's busy, skeptical, and has been burned by agencies before. Be direct, be real, no corporate language.
2. **The story is the pitch.** Baez Auto Repair is the case study. Real numbers, real shop, real results.
3. **Done beats perfect.** Ship fast, iterate based on real feedback.
4. **Don't oversell scope.** Only offer what can actually be delivered right now.
