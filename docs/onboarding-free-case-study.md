# Client Onboarding — Free Case Study

**For the first 2 clients: free in exchange for experience, data, and a testimonial.**

---

## Step 1 — Intake Call (20–30 min)

Confirm scope and set expectations before touching anything.

- Current situation: no website, no GBP, word of mouth only?
- Services offered, hours, location, languages spoken
- Do they have photos? A logo? Any branding?
- **Set the deal in writing:** free in exchange for an honest testimonial + permission to use their results in marketing

---

## Step 2 — Asset Collection (async, 48hr turnaround)

Send a simple checklist via text:

- [ ] 3–5 photos — exterior, bays, team
- [ ] List of services + rough prices
- [ ] Phone number, address, hours
- [ ] Any existing reviews (Google, Yelp, Facebook)

---

## Step 3 — Build (1–2 weeks)

- Domain + Google Workspace setup
- GBP claimed, optimized, photos uploaded
- 5-page website built and deployed
- Contact form or booking link live

---

## Step 4 — Review Call (20 min)

Walk through everything live — on screen or in person.

- Show the site, GBP, how calls and form submissions come in
- Train them: responding to reviews, updating hours, adding photos
- Get verbal approval — record if possible

---

## Step 5 — Baseline Before Go-Live

Document the before-state so you have real numbers for the testimonial.

| Metric | Before |
|--------|--------|
| Calls per week (self-reported) | |
| Google rank for "[city] auto repair" | |
| GBP views/calls | 0 (new listing) |

---

## Step 6 — 30/60-Day Check-in

- Pull GBP insights: calls, direction requests, search views
- Ask: "More calls than before? Roughly how many?"
- Request the testimonial at 60 days with numbers attached

**Ideal format:** 30-second video from their phone. A real shop owner on camera beats any written quote.

---

## Testimonial Ask (script)

> "Hey [name], it's been about 60 days — wanted to check in. Have you noticed more calls or new customers coming in? ... That's great. Would you be willing to record a quick 30-second video for me? Just say who you are, what we did, and what changed. Totally casual, phone is fine. It helps me a lot and I'd really appreciate it."
